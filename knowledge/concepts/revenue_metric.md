# Overview

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) measures normalized monthly subscription revenue generated by active customer contracts.

# Calculation Logic

MRR is calculated by summing normalized monthly fees across active plans in [[concepts/subscription_plans]] and factoring completed transactions from [[concepts/customer_orders]].

```sql
SELECT 
  SUM(p.monthly_rate_usd) AS total_mrr
FROM subscription_plans p
JOIN customer_profiles c ON p.customer_id = c.customer_id
WHERE p.status = 'ACTIVE';
```
