1. DATA CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tier 1 (Public): Non-sensitive marketing materials, product catalog prices.
- Tier 2 (Internal): Aggregated order metrics, anonymized customer profiles.
- Tier 3 (Restricted): Customer PII (Email, Phone, Payment Tokens).

2. RETENTION WINDOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Transaction Records: Retained in BigQuery sales.customer_orders for 7 years.
- Customer Demographics: Retained in PostgreSQL billing.subscription_plans as long
  as account status is ACTIVE. Deleted 90 days after account termination.
