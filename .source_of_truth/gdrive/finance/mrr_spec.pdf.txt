1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & MRR DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) represents the normalized monthly subscription fee
contracted by active customer accounts. It excludes one-time setup fees, usage-based
overage charges, and taxes.

2. FORMULA & COMPUTATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For any active customer subscription plan i:
  MRR_i = (Contract Value USD) / (Contract Duration in Months)

Gross MRR = SUM(MRR_i for all active plans)
Net MRR = Gross MRR + Expansion MRR - Contraction MRR - Churn MRR

3. DATA SOURCES & LINEAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Active subscriptions are queried from PostgreSQL table: billing.subscription_plans
- Transaction payment receipts are verified in BigQuery table: enterprise.sales.orders
- Customer tier metadata is joined via API Endpoint: /v1/customers
